STEPS TO APPLY

1) APPLY (12-6 Months Before Program Start)

Submit application materials • USD $95 fee

  • English language scores not required where English is an official language
  • Unofficial transcripts acceptable for application

2) FINALIZE ADMISSION

If recommended for admission, submit proof of funding and official transcript.

Evaluation service not required.

3) OBTAIN VISA

Receive I-20 document and apply for F-1 student visa.

Allow at least one month for visa processing.

4) PRE-ARRIVAL & ORIENTATION

Attend pre-arrival virtual webinars 1-3 months before program and international orientation the week before classes start.
